Lasagna Recipe

Home

Description

Lasagna is a baked pasta dish made with layers of noodles, savory meat sauce, and creamy cheese. As it bakes, the layers set into a hearty meal with a golden top.

Let the lasagna rest briefly after baking so it is easier to slice and serve.

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 jar (24 ounces) pasta sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C). Cook the noodles according to the package directions, then drain.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat. Drain excess fat, stir in the pasta sauce, and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. In a bowl, mix the ricotta, egg, Italian seasoning, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  4. Spread a little meat sauce in a baking dish. Layer three noodles, one-third of the ricotta mixture, one-third of the remaining sauce, and some mozzarella. Repeat the layers twice.
  5. Top with the remaining mozzarella and Parmesan. Cover the dish with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  6. Remove the foil and bake for another 15 to 20 minutes, until bubbling and golden. Rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
