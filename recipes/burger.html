Description

This classic burger pairs a juicy seasoned beef patty with a soft bun and fresh toppings. Toasting the buns adds a little crunch and helps keep them from getting soggy.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 slices cheddar cheese (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • Lettuce leaves
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • Ketchup, mustard, or mayonnaise

Steps

  1. Divide the ground beef into four portions and gently shape each into a patty. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the oil in a skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Cook the patties for about 4 to 5 minutes per side, until browned and cooked through. Add cheese during the last minute if using.
  4. Toast the buns cut-side down in the pan until lightly golden.
  5. Spread your preferred condiments on the buns. Add lettuce, a patty, tomato, and onion, then top with the other half of the bun.
  6. Serve immediately.
