Butter Chicken

Home

Description

Butter chicken is a rich, comforting curry made with tender chicken simmered in a spiced tomato and cream sauce. It is traditionally served with rice or warm naan.

Marinating the chicken with yogurt and spices helps keep it tender and gives the dish its deep, savory flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 pounds boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
  • 1 can (14 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Cooked rice or naan, for serving

Steps

  1. Mix the yogurt, lemon juice, cumin, garam masala, paprika, and a pinch of salt. Add the chicken and turn to coat. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Melt the butter in a large pan over medium heat. Add the chicken and cook, turning occasionally, until browned and nearly cooked through. Transfer it to a plate.
  3. Add the onion to the pan and cook until softened. Stir in the garlic and ginger and cook for about 30 seconds.
  4. Add the crushed tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Stir in the cream, then return the chicken and any juices to the pan. Simmer for 8 to 10 minutes, until the chicken is fully cooked and the sauce has thickened.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot with rice or naan.
