Biriyani

Home

Description

Biriyani is a fragrant rice dish layered with spiced chicken, herbs, and aromatics. The rice and chicken finish cooking together so their flavors mingle.

This version uses a pot on the stovetop and is served warm with yogurt or a simple salad.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 pounds boneless chicken thighs, cut into pieces
  • 2 cups basmati rice, rinsed
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil or ghee
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • 3 cups chicken broth or water
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Mix the chicken with the yogurt, cumin, garam masala, turmeric, chili powder, and salt. Set aside for 20 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil or ghee in a heavy pot. Cook the sliced onion until golden, then stir in the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the chicken mixture and cook for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the outside of the chicken is no longer raw.
  4. Stir in the rinsed rice and broth. Bring to a boil, then cover and reduce the heat to low.
  5. Cook for 18 to 20 minutes, until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed. Turn off the heat and let the covered pot rest for 10 minutes.
  6. Fluff gently, fold in the cilantro, and serve hot.
